# JAN AI - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
JAN AI collects information to provide better services to all our users.
- **Account Data:** When you sign up, we collect your name and email address.
- **Content Data:** Content generated, uploaded, or analyzed through the app.
- **Usage Data:** Device information, IP address, and interaction metrics.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our AI Marketing Operating System.
- Develop new features, such as enhanced content predictions.
- Provide personalized recommendations via the Founder Assistant.

## 3. Data Protection
We implement industry-standard security measures, including secured backend JWT authentication and Bcrypt password hashing, to protect your data. Your API keys for social networks are encrypted at rest.

## 4. Contact Us
For questions regarding this policy, contact privacy@janai.local.
