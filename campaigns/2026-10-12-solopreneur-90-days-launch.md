## Distribution checklist

- [ ] Review each platform's copy and character limits.
- [ ] Confirm profile/page identity and connected publishing permissions.
- [ ] Use platform-specific media/crops if adding the LinkedIn graphic.
- [ ] Publish manually or through an approved API connector; do not automate browser login or bypass platform controls.
- [ ] Record platform, post URL, published timestamp, and status.
- [ ] Check comments/DMs and respond to relevant contributors.
- [ ] Review results after 48–72 hours: replies, repository visits, qualified contributor conversations, and actual contributions.

## Safety and publishing rules

- Treat the LinkedIn post as the canonical source, but do not copy the same long-form text verbatim everywhere.
- Do not claim that a platform post was published unless the platform confirms success.
- Keep publishing behind an explicit approval step. A prepared draft is not permission to publish.
- Never put access tokens, cookies, passwords, or private account data in this file or in Git.
