# Agency Upgrade Blueprint: JAN AI — Multi-Account Content OS ## 1. Core Objective Transform JAN AI from a single-user tool into a scalable agency-grade platform for managing multiple brands and content teams. ## 2. Hierarchical Architecture - **Workspace:** The agency or organization container. - **Accounts:** Individual brands or clients (e.g., Brand A, Client B). - **Platforms:** Linked social media profiles (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Reddit). - **Content:** The actual posts, threads, and carousels generated and managed within an Account. ## 3. New Backend Services - `account_service.py`: Management of brands, niches, and tones. - `platform_service.py`: Simulated connection layer for social profiles. - `calendar_service.py`: Scheduling logic and time-based recommendations. - `pipeline_service.py`: Workflow management (Draft → Scheduled → Posted). ## 4. Database Expansion - `workspaces`: Agency-level settings and team members. - `accounts`: Brand profiles, niches, target audiences, and goals. - `platform_connections`: Simulated auth and preference storage. - `content_schedule`: Time-slots, frequency rules, and queue management. - `content_pipeline`: Status tracking for every content piece across the workflow. ## 5. UI/UX Strategy - **Workspace Dashboard:** High-level performance across all managed brands. - **Account Manager:** CRUD for brands, niche definitions, and platform linking. - **Content Calendar:** A unified, multi-brand visual schedule with drag-and-drop capability. - **Pipeline Board:** Kanban-style visualization of content status.
