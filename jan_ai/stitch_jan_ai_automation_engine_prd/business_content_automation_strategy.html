# Business Content Automation Strategy: JAN AI ## 1. Core Logic: The Automation Engine JAN AI is a "Business Content Automation Engine," not a simple generator. It transforms raw input into a strategic, multi-platform ecosystem. ## 2. The Extraction & Transformation Flow 1. **Input:** Daily thoughts, idea uploads, or raw notes. 2. **Extraction:** Identifies business type, core topics, and specific intent. 3. **Weekly Plan Engine:** Generates a 7-day strategy (Daily breakdown + platform assignment). 4. **Format Transformation:** One idea → multiple distinct structures (LinkedIn, X Thread, IG Caption, YT Short/Long, Blog, Newsletter). ## 3. Strategic Content Mix The engine balances content to ensure high-impact results: - **40% Value (Educational)** - **30% Storytelling** - **20% Authority** - **10% Selling (Marketing)** ## 4. Business Adaptation Layer Content is filtered through the Account's unique: - **Niche** - **Target Audience** - **Primary Goal** ## 5. Persistence & Automation - **Idea Bank:** Historical storage for future reuse and expansion. - **Auto-Fill Calendar:** Seamless integration from generated plan to schedule.
