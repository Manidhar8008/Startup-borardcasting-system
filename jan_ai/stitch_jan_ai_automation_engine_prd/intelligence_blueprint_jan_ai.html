# Intelligence Blueprint: JAN AI — Content Intelligence Engine ## 1. Core Objective Transform unstructured daily "thought dumps" into structured knowledge, categorized insights, and platform-ready content. ## 2. Processing Pipeline - **Step 1: Extraction:** Pulling topics, intent, tone, and key ideas from raw text. - **Step 2: Categorization:** Classifying into Personal Growth, Business, AI/Tech, etc. - **Step 3: User Profiling:** Updating user interests and style dynamically. - **Step 4: Generation:** Creating specific outputs for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Reddit. - **Step 5: Restructuring:** Converting content into short-form, long-form, threads, and carousels. ## 3. Strategy & Scheduling - **Posting Strategy:** Determining what, where, and when to post based on content type and history. - **Suggestion Engine:** Analyzing performance patterns to suggest new topics and improvement areas. ## 4. Output Requirements - Structured JSON containing extracted data, categorized data, generated content, strategy, and suggestions.
